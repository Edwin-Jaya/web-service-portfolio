Our Story

Where the night begins with coffee.

Noir & Ember started as a one-machine roastery in a basement. Today it's a dark, intimate room where we roast, pull and pour with the same obsession.

By day, precise espresso. By night, low light, slow music and coffee-forward cocktails.
