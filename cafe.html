Our Story

A slower, finer way to begin the day.

Maison Aurelia began with a simple belief: that a cup of coffee deserves patience. We source single-origin beans from small farms, roast in small batches, and bake every pastry before sunrise.

Our space is intentionally quiet — warm light, natural stone, and time to linger.
